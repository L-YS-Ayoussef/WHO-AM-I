# 👨‍💻 Who Am I?

### 🎓 Education & Academic Achievement

- Graduated with a **B.Sc. in Communications and Information Engineering** from the **University of Science and Technology in Zewail City**.
- Graduated with a **3.80 final GPA** and **Magna Cum Laude honors**.
- Built a strong academic foundation across wireless communications, digital signal processing, communication systems, computer networks, machine learning, deep learning, probability, and embedded systems.

### 👨‍🏫 Teaching Experience

- Worked as a **Part-Time Teaching Assistant** for **CSCI 101 – Introduction to Computer Science** at Zewail City.
- Supported undergraduate students in learning **Python programming, problem solving, algorithmic thinking, and debugging**.

### 💻 Software Development Experience

- Have **2+ years of software development experience**, working on real-world software applications and engineering problems.
- Experienced in building **full-stack web applications, backend APIs, database-integrated systems, authentication and authorization workflows, real-time applications, and AI-enabled software systems**.
- Worked across both frontend and backend development using modern software engineering tools, frameworks, APIs, and databases.

### 🔬 Research Experience

#### 🇵🇰 Research Internship Program — NUST, Pakistan

- Completed a **research internship program** at the **National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Islamabad, Pakistan**.
- Worked in **AI/ML for wireless communications**, focusing on multitask deep learning for real-time optimization of rotating-polarization BPSK/QPSK communication links.
- Worked with **AWGN, Rayleigh, and Rician channels**, Monte Carlo simulations, BER prediction, and communication-system optimization under computational and latency constraints.

#### 📡 Research Assistant — Zewail City

- Worked as a **Research Assistant** in the field of **wireless communications, RF modeling, and deep learning**.
- Worked on indoor RF power-map generation using **3D modeling and ray tracing**.
- Contributed to applying **U-Net deep learning models** for fast indoor RF coverage prediction.
